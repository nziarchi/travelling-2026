Biobased Transformations

Seventeen transformation projects — social housing, civic and cultural buildings — delivered, on site or in design.
26 rue Miguel Hidalgo · 75019 Paris · nzi.fr ↓
Foreword

The expected answer was always the same: demolish, start over. We made the opposite choice. Not out of ideology — but by reading what was already there before deciding what was missing.

This is not a retrospective. It is a record of transformations: seventeen projects occupying us in 2025 and 2026 — delivered, on site or in design. Built around one conviction: we build better when we build less.

Transformation, in architecture — not the clean slate, but the careful reading of what is already there. We do not replace; we work with what exists.

NZI · Paris · 2012. These seventeen projects are not the sum of our work — they are its living part today: what is on our desks, on our sites, before our juries.

I — Transformationssubtract and add

Transformations.

Renovation means respecting the building as it is. Transformation means making it something else.

Rue Nollet: a 1930s above-ground car park becomes 83 social housing units. Bertelotte: a 1970s office building becomes 145 student rooms in timber and straw. CMI Issy: a chalk factory becomes a museum of religions and community hub. Saint-Dizier: a Decathlon store becomes a modular multi-sport hall. Coquibus: a school is partly demolished, partly raised, without interrupting class. Langevin: a school is restructured around an oasis courtyard in rammed earth.

Six projects, six subtract/add equations. The gesture is not about conservation — it is programmatic. We remove floor area to find light; we add in timber or straw to change the use. Biobased construction is not an overstated ecological argument: it is the technical condition that makes rapid, light, demountable transformation possible.

II — Public buildingssport · culture · early childhood

Public buildings.

Six public commissions. Six times the same question: how to build something that outlasts its programme?

Python-Duvernois stacks gymnasiums to put sport on the roof. Cœur des Sources brings together a music school, gymnasium and auditorium under a single roof structure. Althéa builds a dance studio in prefabricated timber and installs it in three months. Vauréal welcomes early childhood care in timber structure with stone cladding. Musée du Numérique transforms a cinema into a cultural hub. The Médiathèque des Tours Nuages settles at the foot of Émile Aillaud's towers.

A shared vocabulary: biobased timber, local stone, hemp insulation. No programme catalogue — the same gesture, adapted six times to the city it meets.

III — Reversibilitydesigning for change

Reversibility.

Today's programme is not tomorrow's. We design for that.

The Athletes' Village ÎLOT PB10 was delivered in 2024 as 206 athlete beds. In 2025, without demolishing a single load-bearing partition, it became 37 owner-occupied apartments — the structure was designed for that. The CCBN in Nangis is conceived so that a local government headquarters can become a school, then housing, without heavy demolition.

High environmental quality here is not a label — it is the building's capacity to change use without changing material. CLT timber, regular structural grids, demountable fit-out. Reversibility is a technical decision made at sketch design stage, not an option added at the end.

IV — Housingdwelling — structure and insulation

Housing.

Three scales, three construction methods, one requirement: the building that lasts.

Buzenval: 14 social housing units + nursery on a tight corner plot in Paris 20th — NF Habitat HQE Excellent, Paris Climate Plan. Montreuil: 26 units co-designed with future residents in participatory housing. Belvédère Bordeaux: 15,800 m² timber frame (FOB), 15% timber score, Biosourcé NIV 1, BREEAM — what NZI does beyond the Paris region.

Here, structure and insulation are not a technical detail — they are the commission itself. Three projects demonstrating that environmental quality in housing comes through material — timber, straw, hemp — before energy performance.
